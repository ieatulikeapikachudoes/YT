# Channel: Nothing Personal

Faceless animated explainer shorts (YouTube Shorts + TikTok). Vertical 1080x1920, 45 to 75 seconds, AI voice narration, captions on screen.

## What the channel is
- Explains why people and systems behave the way they do. Cold, clear, a little dark. Said like it is obvious, by someone who has seen it.
- Hits a viewer pain first, then reveals a mechanism (the why), not just a fact.
- Topics the owner wants: human behavior, dating and friendship, money and status, power and history (dictators, cartels, dynasties), AI and the future, internet culture, self improvement without the BS, crypto and DeFi, South Asian diaspora family dynamics (as cold structural analysis, never personal).
- Past shorts: why people ghost; Putin's daily routine; the friendship formula; making friends as an adult is painful; skincare industry is a scam (preacher narrator); AI is the new internet (adapt and you're fine); an AI agent left alone escalates into scams (Dan); a DeFi project that survived by dying (Ramses).
- Competitor reference: ThatGuy style pain shorts. Big claim hook, white text, simple visuals, emotional honesty.
- We are TESTING. Vary topic lane, format, narrator and art style across a batch so the results show what works.

## Script structure (mandatory)
HOOK (claim first, no setup, lands by second 3) -> OPEN LOOP (a question the viewer needs answered) -> BODY (escalating, concrete, every line drawable) -> PAYOFF (answers the loop with a mechanism) -> END (one cold line, no recap, no ask, ideally loops back to the hook).
About 2.6 words per second. 120 to 190 words.

## Hard rules
- Plain words a 14 year old understands. Short sentences. No filler, no hedging, no winking.
- NEVER use em dashes or en dashes. Never say the channel name. No calls to subscribe.
- No real private individuals. Public figures only with checkable facts. Absolute words (every, always, never) only if true.
- No medical dosing, no financial advice framed as advice.

## Visual styles available (pick one per short)
- flat: Kurzgesagt-like flat vector, saturated palette on a dark gradient, soft glows, simple round characters.
- mono: black, white and grey, one accent color for the thing that matters, cinematic.
- ink: white line drawings on black, hand-drawn feel, a single emotional narrator character (ThatGuy style).
- paper: stick figures in black ink on warm off-white paper, one red accent (CGP Grey style).
