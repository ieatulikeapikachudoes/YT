# Research: Poland bracing for Kaliningrad (Nov 2026)

## The operation (CBS News, Nov 3 2026)
https://www.cbsnews.com/news/poland-russia-border-operation-kaliningrad-sabotage-provocation-nato/
- Poland launched a large security operation this week on its border with Kaliningrad. Police chief Marek Boron: runs from Monday Nov 2 until at least Nov 8.
- Extra police, counterterrorism units, and GROM special forces sent to Warmia-Masuria (borders Kaliningrad) and Podlaskie (borders Belarus). A command center at national police HQ.
- Polish and allied intelligence warned of possible sabotage and provocations in the first half of November, including border incursions by armed groups.
- Tusk (Tuesday Nov 3): suspicious movement overnight near Kaliningrad, people with flashlights close to the border. The response "may have prevented" a provocation. He admitted it can never be fully confirmed.
- Context: Aug 2026 fire at a Polish drone and military parts factory; Sept 2026 arson at a Starlink ground station Poland blamed on Russia; repeated Russian drones in NATO airspace. MI5 chief McCallum: Russia is escalating hybrid attacks while staying under the Article 5 line.
- Oct 13: Tusk said a call with NATO commander Grynkewich reinforced his sabotage fears (aircraft provocations, attacks on border crossings).

## The night incident (LIGA / RMF24, Nov 3 2026)
https://news.liga.net/en/politics/news/tusk-said-about-preventing-provocations-on-the-border-with-the-kaliningrad-region-nato-also-warned
- Border Guard saw movement just after midnight Nov 3 near Sepopol, Warmia-Masuria. The border was not crossed; the group withdrew into Kaliningrad.
- Tusk: the operation does not mean "tragic events" were avoided; Poland must "remain very focused."
- The day before, Territorial Defence Forces were forming a new brigade for the northern border, with patrols along Kaliningrad.
- No numbers of people or troops published.

## The Suwalki Gap (RTE, Sept 21 2025)
https://www.rte.ie/news/analysis-and-comment/2025/0921/1534438-suwalki-gap-nato-russia
- The Poland-Lithuania border runs about 100 km from the Belarus tripoint to Kaliningrad; 65 km in a straight line. Flanked by Kaliningrad on one side and Belarus on the other.
- The only land link between the three Baltic NATO states and the rest of the alliance.
- Hills, forests, lakes, bogs: tank columns would have to use its two main roads.
- Kaliningrad: about 12,000 Russian troops (estimates vary); Iskander missiles, range up to 500 km. Polish analyst Damian Szacawa: Russia's westernmost outpost, "an island within NATO territory."
- Belarus side: two training grounds, one 3 km from the Polish border, one near Grodno about 25 km from Poland and 1 km from Lithuania.
- NATO has had four multinational battlegroups in Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania and Poland since 2017.
- Szacawa: Sweden and Finland in NATO reduce the gap's importance (Gotland is about 150 km from Latvia, so sea and air resupply works). Direct confrontation unlikely while Russia fights in Ukraine; the more likely play is psychological, "anxiety is what Russia has been trying to create."
- The term was coined in 2015 by Estonian president Ilves; US general Ben Hodges called it one of the most volatile points on the map.
