# Law 46 Weak Ties: sources
- Granovetter, 1970 dissertation "Changing Jobs" (basis of "The Strength of Weak Ties", 1973): 282 professional, technical and managerial workers in Newton, MA. Of the 54 who found their job through a personal contact: 16.7% saw the contact often, 55.6% occasionally, 27.8% rarely. Script line: "Only one in six saw that person often." Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interpersonal_ties
- Rajkumar, Saint-Jacques, Bojinov, Brynjolfsson, Aral, 2022, Science, "A Causal Test of the Strength of Weak Ties": randomized experiments on LinkedIn People You May Know, about 20 million users over five years, about 2 billion new ties, 600,000 job changes. Moderately weak ties produced the most job mobility (inverted U). Script line: "Not distant contacts. Not best friends. The people in between." Source: https://ide.mit.edu/insights/new-study-proves-that-weak-ties-have-strong-employment-value
- Book note for Simon: chapter 46 says Granovetter "surveyed 282 American men who had recently found jobs through personal contacts". Actually 282 workers were surveyed and 54 of them found their job through a contact; the 16.7% is out of those 54.
