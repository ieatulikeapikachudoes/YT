NOTHING PERSONAL

A faceless YouTube channel. One stick figure explains how the world actually is.

The viewer should walk away thinking "this is true and no one told me," and it should be explained simply enough that anyone gets it.

The character
An older man in a suit, glasses, and a KGB pin. No name, just the guy. He's eccentric, wise, and has lived enough to know how things really work. He explains everything as if it's the most obvious thing in the world, even when it isn't. The pin is never mentioned or explained.

He is the only voice. He talks straight, mostly deadpan, with light humor thrown in. He makes a bold claim first, then backs it up with theory, studies, and stories. The stories are both his own and other people's.

Topics
Anything. The theme is how the world actually is. The only test is whether he can say it like it's obvious and back it up.

Format
Shorts first, posted to YouTube Shorts and TikTok to test topics and hooks. Winners get expanded into long form.

Still open, settle by making things and looking
- What's around him in the frame (empty, scene per beat, or diagrams). Pull frames from Ink Explainer, EverythingProfessor, and CGP Grey and mock him up in each.
- How a video opens, and whether there's a recurring line. Draft two versions of the first short and pick.

How to work with me
Plain prose. Short replies unless I ask for a deliverable. No caveats or disclaimers. No em dashes. Give me the draft, not a plan for the draft. Don't lock in decisions I haven't made; ask.
